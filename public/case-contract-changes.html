Case study 03
In production since Apr 2026 HubSpot · Deel · Stripe · n8n · Netlify

Ninety minutes to five, and the cents stopped being wrong

A pay rate increase touched three systems and seven manual steps. Ten of them at once, during an annual salary review, cost the Success team an hour and a half of record creation plus another half hour of contract updates. The calculation behind it was documented in a Finance memo, which is to say it was documented nowhere useful.

02 · Two bugs worth the whole project

Converting a monthly increase into a Deel rate has rules: hourly contracts use a monthly hours multiplier, fixed contracts divide by two for biweekly, part time needs proportional scaling. At least three people had received incorrect values before the tool existed. Cents look small until they repeat every month, and until you remember they land in someone's pay.

03 · Where my responsibility started and stopped
Accountable
Architecture, both API endpoints, all three workflows, the front end, documentation, training.
Consulted
Every pricing rule belongs to Finance. I wrote them down, modelled them, and pushed back when two statements contradicted each other. I did not invent a single billing rule.
Informed
Contract data entry sits with another team. Wrong source data still produces wrong output, and this tool does not solve that.
